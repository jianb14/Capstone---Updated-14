How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and holidays. Rush bookings may still be accommodated depending on schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Metro Manila and nearby areas. For events outside our usual coverage, just send us a message and we'll see how we can help.

How does the GCash downpayment work?

After booking, you'll receive GCash payment details. A downpayment secures your date, and the balance is settled before or on your event day.

Can I customize an existing package?

Yes! You can add addons, adjust colors and themes, and use our design canvas to visualize your setup before confirming your booking.

What happens if it rains or the venue changes?

Let us know as early as possible and we'll work with you on rescheduling or adjusting the setup to fit your new venue or indoor alternatives.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made at least 7 days before the event are eligible for a refund of the downpayment minus processing fees. Check our Terms & Conditions for full details.
