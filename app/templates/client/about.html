01.
The Beginning

Every celebration starts with a spark.

Balloorina.ph was created from a simple love for styling celebrations. What began in 2020 as a small home studio grew from one wish — to make every moment feel special, no matter the size of the event.

02.
Learning the Craft

Listening before styling.

Before every setup, we take time to understand the occasion — the theme, the colors, and the story behind the celebration — so every balloon design feels personal and true to the client.

03.
Growing the Dream

From a small studio to a trusted team.

Word of mouth carried our work from intimate family parties to weddings and corporate events across Metro Manila — one beautifully styled setup at a time.

04.
Where We Are Now

Styling moments that matter.

Today, Balloorina.ph continues to craft clean, elegant, and photo-worthy setups, helping hundreds of clients celebrate life's biggest moments with confidence and style.
